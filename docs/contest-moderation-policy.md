# Contest moderation (community review)

New contests are submitted in a **pending** state. They appear in public search and listings only after an **administrator approves** them. Moderators may **reject** a submission and optionally leave a short note for the organizer.

This process is **community moderation** to keep listings appropriate for the site. It is **not legal advice** and does not create a lawyer–client relationship. Organizers remain responsible for their events and compliance with applicable laws and venue rules.
